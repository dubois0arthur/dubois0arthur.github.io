Engineering Challenges & Troubleshooting

Limited Internal Diameter

Problem: The PVC tube used as the controller body had a very small internal diameter, leaving little room for electronics, wiring, and structural supports.

Solution: Designed compact modular inserts that maximize radial space usage. Components were arranged along the tube axis and stacked in a modular architecture to avoid lateral expansion.

Preventing Internal Movement

Problem: Electronics and modules could shift inside the tube, leading to unreliable sensing and structural instability.

Solution: Designed interlocking 3D-printed modules that lock together and press against the tube walls, preventing movement while still allowing assembly and disassembly.

Maintaining Constant Module Spacing

Problem: Sensor modules required fixed spacing along the tube to maintain consistent sensing geometry and interaction behavior.

Solution: Implemented spacer elements and alignment features in the CAD design to enforce consistent distances between modules during assembly.

Unobtrusive Attachment

Problem: External components such as sensors and LED elements needed to be mounted without interfering with user interaction or the controller's ergonomics.

Solution: Designed slide-on holding zones that keep LDR sensors and LED strips secure but visually and physically unobtrusive.
